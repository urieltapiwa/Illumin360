# Vision & Scope (summary)

> **Status:** Draft · **Owner:** _TBD_ · **Last updated:** 2026-05-29 · **Related ADRs:** —

**Problem.** Businesses struggle to find and rank suitable candidates quickly; professionals/students lack a
trusted channel to stay discoverable.
**Goal.** A compliant, observable, AI-assisted talent platform across five portals.
**Non-goals (initial).** Full ATS replacement; multi-tenant white-label; non-region labour markets.
**Success metrics.** Time-to-shortlist, match acceptance rate, subscription conversion, SLO adherence
(see `06-operations/slo-sli.md`). Full detail: canonical `01_Project_Initiation/` + `_v2.0_refresh/`.

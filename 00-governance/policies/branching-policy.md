# Branching & Commit Policy

> **Status:** Accepted · **Owner:** Platform Architecture · **Last updated:** 2026-05-29 · **Related ADRs:** —

- **Trunk-based** development; short-lived feature branches off `main`.
- **Conventional Commits** (`feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `refactor`, `test`, `chore`, `build`, `ci`).
- PR requires: green CI, CODEOWNERS review, satisfied Definition of Done (charter Part 19).
- SemVer + Keep-a-Changelog; releases tagged `vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`.

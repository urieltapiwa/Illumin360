Isolation & registration

Each portal signs in against its own realm — no cross-portal SSO. Services trust all six issuers (per-realm JWT handlers keyed on iss). Self-registration for student / professional / business / employer runs through the illumin360-registration service client in each realm. Self-service actions are gated by role policies — StudentPolicy, ProfessionalPolicy, EmployerPolicy, SupportPolicy (each its role + admins) — so end users manage their own data. The legacy illumin360 realm now backs only the gateway client (the SPA/BFF that used it have been retired).
